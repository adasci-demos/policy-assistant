# S-01 Scope and definitions

This policy applies to every shipment that Harbourline Freight accepts from a customer, whether the customer is a private individual or a business account holder. It covers parcels, pallets and oversized freight moved by road, rail, sea or air, and it applies to staff in every depot, call centre and sales office.

In this policy, a "customer" is the person or business that booked and paid for the shipment. The "recipient" is the person named on the delivery address. A "parcel" is any item up to 30 kg that fits within the standard size limits. "Freight" means anything heavier or larger than a parcel. A shipment is "domestic" when the collection address and the delivery address are in the same country, and "international" when they are in different countries.

"Dispatch" is the moment a shipment leaves the first Harbourline depot. "Delivery" is the moment the recipient, or a safe place named by the recipient, receives it. "Business days" are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in the country of the customer.

Where this policy and a signed business contract disagree, the signed contract wins. Staff who are unsure which rule applies should ask their team lead before they promise anything to a customer.
