# S-06 Hazardous goods

Hazardous goods include lithium batteries shipped on their own, flammable liquids, aerosols, compressed gases, corrosive cleaning products, paints and solvents. Every hazardous item must be declared by the customer at booking, with its type, quantity and packaging. Staff must never accept an undeclared hazardous item. If an undeclared hazardous item is found at a depot, the shipment is stopped, the safety officer is informed, and the customer is charged a handling fee.

Declared hazardous goods travel only by road and rail. They are never sent by air. They must be packed in approved packaging with hazard labels on two sides. Harbourline does not ship explosives, radioactive material or infectious substances under any circumstances.

Hazardous goods are never refundable after dispatch. Once a hazardous shipment has left the first depot, the delivery fee and any item value are not refunded, even if the customer cancels or the recipient refuses the delivery. This rule overrides the normal refund rules. Hazardous goods also cannot be returned through the normal returns process; a refused hazardous shipment is taken to a licensed disposal site and the disposal cost is charged to the customer.

Staff who are unsure whether an item is hazardous must ask the safety officer before accepting it.
