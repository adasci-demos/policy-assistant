# S-04 Damaged items

If a shipment arrives damaged, for example broken, cracked, crushed or wet, the customer or the recipient must report the damage within 7 days of delivery. The report must include at least one clear photo of the damaged item and one photo of the packaging. Reports without a photo are not accepted, and staff must ask the customer to send one before opening a damage case.

Damage reports can be made in the customer portal or by phone. Staff open a damage case with the tracking number, the photos and a short description. The customer should keep the item and the packaging until the case is closed, because Harbourline may ask to inspect them or to collect them.

When the damage case is accepted, the customer can choose a repair, a replacement shipment at no charge, or a refund of the item value up to the declared value on the booking. Return shipping for a damaged item is free. Reports made after 7 days are refused, unless the damage could not have been seen from the outside, in which case a team lead may accept the report up to 14 days after delivery.

Damage to items that were badly packed by the customer is not covered.
