# S-07 Refund methods and timing

When a refund is approved, Harbourline Freight pays it back to the original payment method used for the booking, such as the same card or the same bank account. Refunds are not paid in cash and are not paid to a different person.

Refunds are paid within 10 business days of approval. A return refund is approved when the returned item is scanned back at a Harbourline depot. A damage refund is approved when the damage case is accepted. A lost parcel refund is approved when the claim is accepted. The customer receives an email when the refund is approved and a second email when it is paid.

For business account holders who pay by invoice, refunds are not paid to a card. Instead, the refund is added as a credit note on the next invoice, within the same 10 business days.

The delivery fee is refunded only when the shipment was lost, damaged in transit, delivered late under the Guaranteed Time option, or never dispatched. For a normal return the delivery fee is not refunded, only the item value. Some goods are never refundable after dispatch; see the hazardous goods rules.

If a refund has not arrived after 10 business days, staff should check the refund status in the payments system before escalating.
