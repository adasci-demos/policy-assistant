# S-05 International shipments and customs

For every international shipment the customer must complete a customs declaration in the booking system. The declaration lists each item, its value, its country of origin and the reason for sending it, such as sale, gift, sample or return. Shipments without a complete customs declaration are not dispatched.

Import duties and taxes are charged by the destination country, not by Harbourline. By default the recipient pays them on delivery. The customer can choose the Duties Paid option when booking, in which case Harbourline charges the estimated duties to the customer in advance.

When an international shipment is returned, the customer must mark the new customs declaration as a return and quote the original tracking number, so that duties are not charged twice. Harbourline cannot refund duties or taxes that were already paid to a customs authority; the customer must claim them back from that authority.

Some items are restricted or banned in certain countries. Staff must check the destination country rules shown in the booking system before accepting a shipment. Delays caused by customs inspections do not count towards late delivery. A shipment held by customs for more than 30 days is treated as a customs hold, and the customer is contacted to decide whether it is returned or abandoned.
