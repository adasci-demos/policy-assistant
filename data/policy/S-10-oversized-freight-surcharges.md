# S-10 Oversized freight surcharges

A shipment is oversized when any side is longer than 120 cm, when the length plus the girth is more than 300 cm, or when a single item weighs more than 30 kg. Oversized shipments are carried as freight and pay an oversized surcharge on top of the normal delivery price.

The oversized surcharge is 25 for items up to 70 kg and 60 for items from 70 kg to 150 kg, in the local currency. Items heavier than 150 kg need a pallet booking and a quote from the freight team. A two-person delivery is required for items over 70 kg and costs an extra 20.

If a customer books a shipment as a parcel and it is measured as oversized at the depot, Harbourline adds the surcharge to the booking and emails the customer. The shipment continues without waiting for approval, unless the extra charge is more than 100, in which case it is held until the customer agrees.

Oversized freight is not available with Express or Economy delivery. It always travels with Standard delivery and takes 1 extra business day on top of the standard delivery time. Oversized freight cannot be left in a safe place and must be signed for.
