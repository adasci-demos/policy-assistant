# S-02 Standard delivery times

Harbourline Freight offers three delivery speeds. Express delivery arrives on the next business day for domestic parcels booked before 2 pm. Standard delivery arrives within 2 to 4 business days for domestic parcels. Economy delivery arrives within 5 to 7 business days for domestic parcels and is only offered for parcels, not freight.

International parcels take 5 to 10 business days with standard delivery, depending on the destination and on customs clearance. Express international delivery takes 2 to 4 business days. Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees, unless the customer bought the Guaranteed Time option, which refunds the delivery fee when the parcel arrives late.

The delivery time starts at dispatch, not at booking. Parcels booked on a weekend are dispatched on the next business day. Delivery attempts are made between 8 am and 6 pm. If nobody is available, the driver leaves a card and makes a second attempt on the next business day. After two failed attempts the parcel is held at the nearest depot for 10 calendar days, and the recipient can collect it with photo identification.

Staff should give customers the delivery estimate shown in the booking system and should not promise a faster time.
