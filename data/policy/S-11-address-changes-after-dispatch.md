# S-11 Address changes after dispatch

Before dispatch, the customer can change the delivery address in the customer portal at no charge. After dispatch, an address change is called a redirect and is possible only for domestic shipments.

A redirect after dispatch costs a fee of 8 in the local currency and adds 1 to 2 business days to the delivery time. The new address must be in the same country. Only the customer who booked the shipment can request a redirect; the recipient can instead choose a safe place or a pickup point for collection.

International shipments cannot be redirected after dispatch because the customs declaration names the delivery address. For an international shipment the customer must wait for delivery, or ask for the shipment to be returned, which is charged as a new shipment.

A shipment can be redirected only once. Redirect requests are not accepted once the shipment is out for delivery on the delivery day. Staff must check the caller's identity with the booking email and the tracking number before changing any address. If a redirected shipment is later lost, the lost parcel rules apply, with the tracking limit counted from the date of the redirect instead of the dispatch date.
