# S-09 Business accounts and invoices

Businesses that ship regularly can open a Harbourline business account. A business account gives volume discounts, a dedicated account manager and the option to pay by monthly invoice instead of paying for each booking by card.

Invoices are issued on the first business day of each month and cover every shipment dispatched in the previous month. Payment is due within 30 days of the invoice date. If an invoice is not paid within 30 days, a reminder is sent, and if it is still unpaid after 45 days, new bookings on the account are paused until the balance is paid.

Business customers can dispute an invoice line within 60 days of the invoice date by contacting their account manager. While a dispute is open, the disputed amount does not need to be paid, but the rest of the invoice does. Credits for refunds, damage cases and accepted claims are shown as credit notes on the next invoice.

Each business account can have up to 20 named users. Only the account owner can add or remove users, change the billing address, or close the account. Staff must check that a caller is a named user on the account before sharing invoice details.
