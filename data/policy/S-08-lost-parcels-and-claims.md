# S-08 Lost parcels and claims

A shipment is treated as lost when its tracking has not been updated for 10 business days for a domestic shipment, or 20 business days for an international shipment. Before that point, staff should open a trace request, not a claim.

To make a lost parcel claim, the customer submits a claim form in the customer portal within 60 days of the dispatch date. The claim must include the tracking number, a description of the contents, and proof of value such as a receipt or an invoice. Claims made more than 60 days after dispatch are refused.

Harbourline pays lost parcel claims up to the declared value on the booking. If no value was declared, the maximum payment is 100 per parcel in the local currency. Customers who bought Extended Cover can claim up to the extended cover amount instead.

If a parcel that was claimed as lost is found later, it is delivered to the recipient as normal. If the claim was already paid, the customer can either keep the payment and let Harbourline keep the parcel, or return the payment and receive the parcel.

Staff must never promise a claim outcome on the phone; only the claims team can accept or refuse a claim.
