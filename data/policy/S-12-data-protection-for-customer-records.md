# S-12 Data protection for customer records

Customer records include names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, payment details and shipment history. Staff may open a customer record only to handle a shipment, a claim or a request from that customer.

Staff must never share a customer's personal data with anyone else by phone, email or chat, including the recipient, unless the customer has given permission in the booking. A recipient may be told the tracking status of a shipment but not the customer's address, phone number or payment details. Staff must never send full card numbers by email or chat, and must never write customer data on paper notes.

Customer records are kept for 6 years after the last shipment, for tax and legal reasons, and are then deleted. A customer can ask for a copy of their data or ask for it to be corrected. These requests go to the data protection team and must be answered within 30 days. Staff should log the request and pass it on the same day.

Any suspected data breach, such as an email sent to the wrong customer or a lost laptop, must be reported to the data protection team within 24 hours. Staff must not try to investigate a data breach on their own.
